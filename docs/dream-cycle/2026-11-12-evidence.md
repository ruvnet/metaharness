## INFERENCE
The bare point-estimate gate was strictly weaker than what ADR-155 documents the system as doing — it
could, in principle, certify "beyond SOTA vs all baselines" on a razor-thin, statistically-insignificant
margin for B0/B1 while only B2 got real certification. No evidence this has happened in production runs to
date (the regenerated snapshot shows all three margins are comfortably positive under the current corpus),
but the gate offered no protection against it.
