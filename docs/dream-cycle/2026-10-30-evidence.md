## INFERENCE

`publish_readiness` is the dominant term of `genome.ts`'s `risk_score`
(weight 0.5 of 1.0, vs. 0.3 for MCP surface and 0.2 for test confidence) and
directly gates the `ready`/`needs-work`/`blocked` verdict at the `publish >=
0.75 && risk < 0.35` boundary. A repo whose only "test" signal is a
language-inferred manifest entry (no real test files) could therefore clear
the "ready" bar and exit 0 — the exact false-positive class three sibling
scorers in this package already closed for their own dimensions
(`scoreTestConfidence`, merged #229 2026-08-25; `score.ts::scoreTestCoverage`
and `repo-scorecard.ts::compileConfidence`, both fixed on still-unmerged
branches). This was the one holdout, and the highest-stakes member of the
family since it alone drives the process exit code.
