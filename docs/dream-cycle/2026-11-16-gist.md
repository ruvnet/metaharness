# Flywheel-Promotion SOTA Report — 2026-11-16

TL;DR: `verifyMultiGenerationEvidence()` (`@metaharness/flywheel`, ADR-318) classifies a multi-generation
self-improvement chain into one of five increasing-trust proof tiers. Its final classification step used
`e.generations.every((g) => g.evidenceClass === 'synthetic')` to choose between the two middle tiers — and
`evidenceClass` itself was never validated against its own 4-value enum anywhere in the function. The
result: a chain that mixes one real `'synthetic'` label into otherwise-`'development'` evidence, or that
carries a typo'd/missing `evidenceClass` on any generation, was upgraded past where its weakest evidence
actually supports it — landing on `BOUNDED_RECURSIVE_IMPROVER` (ADR-318's second-strongest tier) with
`pass: true` and zero failures, instead of being downgraded (mixed labels) or rejected outright (invalid
label). Fixed by (1) validating `evidenceClass` against its enum per generation, failing closed to
`INVALID` on anything unrecognized, and (2) switching the synthetic-vs-bounded decision from unanimous
(`every`) to weakest-link (`some`).

## MetaHarness Current Capability

`verifyMultiGenerationEvidence()` is the ADR-318 verifier distinguishing mechanism-only evidence from a
genuine recursive-improver claim, gated in CI (`.github/workflows/multigeneration-proof.yml` runs its unit
tests plus `scripts/benchmark-multigeneration-proof.mjs`, a 10,000-case synthetic adversarial benchmark,
on every PR touching this file). It is not a dead/unwired primitive — real CI depends on it — which is
part of why a validation gap in its own trust-tier classification matters.

## Hypothesis

Given a `MultiGenerationEvidence` chain that otherwise satisfies every structural, capability, control, and
reviewer-independence check `verifyMultiGenerationEvidence()` enforces, when (a) one generation's
`evidenceClass` is `'synthetic'` while the others are `'development'`/`'sealed_holdout'`, or (b) any
generation's `evidenceClass` is missing or not one of the four defined values, then the verdict should be,
respectively, (a) `SYNTHETIC_RECURSIVE_IMPROVER` (the weakest generation determines the whole chain's
tier) or (b) `pass: false` / `proofClass: 'INVALID'` with an `invalid_evidence_class` failure — not, as
before the fix, `BOUNDED_RECURSIVE_IMPROVER` with `pass: true` and zero failures in both cases. Frozen
before implementation, confirmed via a live `node` repro against the pre-fix built `dist/cjs` output
before writing the `src/` fix; not revised after evaluation.
