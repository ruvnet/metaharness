## INFERENCE

the other 4 dimensions whenever the MCP-dimension score stays above ~40-50
(any combination where shell is allowed but audit/network/filewrite are
correctly gated stays as high as 85/100 despite `mcpRisk: 'High'`), meaning
badge pairs like "Grade A, MCP Risk: High" were reachable in production
output, not merely a contrived edge case.
